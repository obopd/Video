# Video

`output.mp4` is a 15-second vertical (720x1280, 30fps) music video that was generated entirely in code: no camera, no stock footage, no AI video model.

- **Visuals:** a warp-speed starfield, then a plasma field with orbiting rings that pulse on the beat, then a synthwave sunset with a scrolling grid.
- **Audio:** synthesized from scratch. It has a pad chord progression (Am–F–C–G), bass, an arpeggio, a kick and hi-hats at 100 BPM.

Regenerate it with:

```
python3 generate.py output.mp4   # requires numpy, Pillow, ffmpeg
```

## Axol: a rigged character animation

`axol.mp4` is 30 seconds at 1080x1920 and 30fps. It's generated by `axol.py`, which needs `pip install pycairo`.

Axol is a skeleton redrawn from scratch every frame, not a sprite that gets moved around:

- **Legs:** two-bone IK keeps the feet planted while the body moves.
- **Arms:** shoulder and elbow joints.
- **Head:** tilts on the neck and turns toward a 3/4 view.
- **Tail:** a 14-segment chain.
- **Gills:** six of them.
- **Face:** the eyes blink and have a happy and a sleepy look. The mouth can smile, open and yawn.

**Secondary motion:** damped springs make the gills and tail lag and wobble on their own.

**Actions:** each one is posed with eased keyframes, uses squash and stretch, and blends into the next. In order: idle and look around, wave, walk, turn and walk back, jump with a backflip, dance, swim along a curved path, and yawn and fall asleep. Sound effects play on footsteps, the jump, the landing, bubbles and the yawn.
